## Install

**Homebrew**

```sh
brew install --cask tauruslabsofficial-tech/tap/earshare
```

**Or download** [EarShare.dmg](https://github.com/tauruslabsofficial-tech/EarShare-releases/releases/latest/download/EarShare.dmg), open it and drag EarShare into Applications.

> EarShare isn't notarized by Apple yet. If you install from the DMG, macOS asks you to allow it once: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. The Homebrew cask skips that step.

EarShare **updates itself**: it checks once a day and offers each new version with one click.

## Use

1. Connect each pair of Bluetooth headphones to your Mac. New headphones are selected automatically.
2. Press **Start Sharing**, or flip the switch in the menu bar.
3. Play anything: Netflix, YouTube, Safari, Chrome, VLC, the TV app.
4. Press **Stop Sharing**, or quit, to go back to your normal output.

Each person gets their own volume slider and mute.

## Why EarShare

- **No added lag.** Sound goes straight through macOS's own audio engine. Each pair sounds exactly as it does on its own.
- **Any mix of headphones.** AirPods, Sony WH-1000XM5, Bose, Beats and more.
- **Hands-off reliability.** Headphones that drop out rejoin automatically, and sharing recovers after sleep. Quitting always restores your normal output.
- **Feels at home on your Mac.** Native design, Liquid Glass on macOS 26, light and dark mode.
- **Nothing extra to install.** No drivers, extensions or virtual audio devices.
